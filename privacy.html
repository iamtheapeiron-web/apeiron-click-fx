APEIRON Click FX — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

APEIRON Click FX is a Twitch Extension that provides viewer-side visual effects directly in the Twitch video player.

Data Collection

APEIRON Click FX does not require users to create an account and does not intentionally collect personal information such as names, email addresses, payment information, or private messages.

Twitch Data

The Extension operates within Twitch and may receive technical information made available by the Twitch Extensions platform as necessary for the Extension to function.

Local Storage

The Extension may store viewer preferences, such as whether visual effects are enabled, locally in the user's browser.

Streamer Configuration

Streamer settings for the Extension may be stored using Twitch's Extensions Configuration Service.

Third Parties

APEIRON Click FX does not sell personal data and does not use third-party advertising or analytics services.
